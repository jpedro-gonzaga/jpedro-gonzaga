## About me

I'm **João Pedro Sá**, a Computer Engineering student at the **Federal University of Sergipe (UFS)** and the developer behind **[ZAUPEN](https://zaupen.com.br)** — a study ecosystem that brings planning, focus, spaced review and progress analysis into one product.

- I build end-to-end SaaS products, from **React and TypeScript interfaces** to **Node.js and TypeScript APIs** backed by PostgreSQL.
- My academic work spans **software engineering, operating systems, data engineering, networks, computer architecture and digital systems**.
- I care about useful products, clear interfaces and software that remains reliable as it grows.
